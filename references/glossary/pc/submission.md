# Submission Terms

## Submission

A pre-policy insurance request or application that is being evaluated for quote, bind, decline, withdrawal, or later policy issuance.

## Submission Identifier

A stable canonical identifier for a submission record.

## Submission Number

A business-facing number used to identify a submission.

## Submission Status

The current lifecycle state of a submission.

## Submission Type

A classification of the kind of submission being evaluated.

## Received Date

The date when the submission was received for intake or evaluation.

## Requested Effective Date

The date when the requester wants coverage or policy activity to become effective.

## Quote Date

The date when a quote or price indication is provided for a submission.

## Bind Date

The date when coverage is bound or committed for a submission, subject to the applicable business process.

## Decline Date

The date when the submission is declined.

## Withdrawn Date

The date when the submission is withdrawn from consideration.

## Related Policy

A policy connected to a submission, usually after bind, issue, renewal, or another policy-forming activity.

## Submission Party Role

The participation of a party in a submission context.

## Submission Risk

Risk information presented or evaluated as part of a submission.

## Requested Coverage

Coverage requested or indicated for a submitted risk.

## Submission Assessment

An underwriting, triage, clearance, referral, or risk evaluation activity performed within a submission.

## Assessment Type

A classification of the kind of assessment performed on a submission or submitted risk.

## Assessment Status

The current lifecycle state of a submission assessment.

## Assessment Result

The outcome or conclusion of a submission assessment.

## Referral

An indication that a submission or submitted risk requires review by a person, team, or process beyond the initial handling path.

## Assessment Summary

A concise business summary of the assessment result or rationale.

## Submission Document

A document associated with a submission, such as supporting information, forms, correspondence, or issued material.

## Document Reference

A source-neutral pointer or reference to where a document can be found or retrieved.

## Required Document

A document expected or required for submission evaluation or completion.

## Submission Lifecycle Event

A meaningful event that changes or records progress in the submission lifecycle.

## Prior Status

The lifecycle status before a lifecycle event occurred.

## Resulting Status

The lifecycle status after a lifecycle event occurred.

## Initiating Party

The party that started, requested, or caused a lifecycle event.
